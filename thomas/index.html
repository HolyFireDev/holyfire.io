Holy Fire / the gospel of thomas

The Gospel of Thomas

Translated from the Sahidic Coptic of Nag Hammadi Codex II,2 (pages 32 to 51). The rendering is kept literal. Words in square brackets are restored where the manuscript is damaged. Where the Greek fragments from Oxyrhynchus (P. Oxy. 1, 654 and 655) differ in substance, a note says so. The Coptic text follows the Coptic Scriptorium transcription. November 2026.

These are the hidden words that the living Jesus spoke, and Didymos Judas Thomas wrote them down.

1And he said: Whoever finds the meaning of these words will not taste death.

2Jesus said: Let the one who seeks not stop seeking until he finds. And when he finds, he will be shaken; and when he is shaken, he will marvel, and he will be king over the all. (The Greek adds: and having been king, he will rest.)

3Jesus said: If those who lead you say to you, "Look, the kingdom is in the sky," then the birds of the sky will be there before you. If they say to you, "It is in the sea," then the fish will be there before you. But the kingdom is within you and it is outside you. When you know yourselves, then you will be known, and you will understand that you are the children of the living father. But if you do not know yourselves, then you are in poverty, and you are the poverty.

4Jesus said: The man old in days will not hesitate to ask a small child of seven days about the place of life, and he will live. For many who are first will be last, and they will become a single one.

5Jesus said: Know what is in front of your face, and what is hidden from you will be uncovered to you. For there is nothing hidden that will not be revealed. (The Greek adds: and nothing buried that will not be raised.)

6His disciples asked him and said to him: Do you want us to fast? And how should we pray? Should we give alms? And what food should we keep from? Jesus said: Do not lie, and what you hate, do not do, for all things are uncovered before heaven. For there is nothing hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing covered that will stay without being uncovered.

7Jesus said: Blessed is the lion that a man will eat, and the lion becomes man; and cursed is the man whom the lion will eat, and the lion will become man. (The manuscript repeats "the lion will become man"; editors often emend the second to "the man will become lion.")

8And he said: The man is like a wise fisherman who cast his net into the sea; he drew it up from the sea full of small fish. Among them the wise fisherman found a large good fish. He threw all the small fish back into the sea and chose the large fish without trouble. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.

9Jesus said: Look, the sower went out, filled his hand, and threw. Some fell on the road; the birds came and gathered them. Others fell on the rock and did not send a root down into the earth or raise ears to the sky. Others fell among thorns; they choked the seed and the worm ate them. And others fell on the good earth and gave good fruit up to the sky: it bore sixty per measure and a hundred and twenty per measure.

10Jesus said: I have thrown fire on the world, and look, I am watching over it until it burns.

11Jesus said: This sky will pass away, and the one above it will pass away. The dead are not alive, and the living will not die. In the days when you ate what is dead, you made it alive. When you come to be in the light, what will you do? On the day when you were one, you became two. But when you become two, what will you do?

12The disciples said to Jesus: We know you will go away from us. Who will be great over us? Jesus said to them: Wherever you have come from, you will go to James the Just, for whose sake sky and earth came to be.

13Jesus said to his disciples: Compare me; tell me whom I am like. Simon Peter said to him: You are like a just angel. Matthew said to him: You are like a wise philosopher. Thomas said to him: Teacher, my mouth is wholly unable to say whom you are like. Jesus said: I am not your teacher; because you drank, you became drunk from the bubbling spring that I measured out. And he took him, withdrew, and said three words to him. When Thomas came back to his companions, they asked him: What did Jesus say to you? Thomas said to them: If I tell you one of the words he said to me, you will pick up stones and throw them at me, and fire will come out of the stones and burn you.

14Jesus said to them: If you fast, you will bring sin on yourselves; and if you pray, you will be condemned; and if you give alms, you will do harm to your spirits. And when you go into any land and walk in the districts, if they receive you, eat what they set before you, and heal the sick among them. For what goes into your mouth will not defile you; what comes out of your mouth, that is what will defile you.

15Jesus said: When you see one who was not born of woman, fall on your faces and worship him. That one is your father.

16Jesus said: Perhaps people think I have come to throw peace on the world, and they do not know that I have come to throw divisions on the earth: fire, sword, war. For there will be five in a house: three will be against two and two against three, the father against the son and the son against the father, and they will stand as solitary ones.

17Jesus said: I will give you what no eye has seen and what no ear has heard and what no hand has touched and what has not come up into the heart of man.

18The disciples said to Jesus: Tell us how our end will be. Jesus said: Have you then uncovered the beginning, that you seek the end? For where the beginning is, there the end will be. Blessed is the one who will stand at the beginning: he will know the end, and he will not taste death.

19Jesus said: Blessed is the one who was before he came to be. If you become my disciples and listen to my words, these stones will serve you. For you have five trees in paradise that do not move in summer or winter and whose leaves do not fall. Whoever knows them will not taste death.

20The disciples said to Jesus: Tell us what the kingdom of the skies is like. He said to them: It is like a mustard seed, the smallest of all seeds. But when it falls on tilled ground, it puts out a great branch and becomes shelter for the birds of the sky.

21Mary said to Jesus: Whom are your disciples like? He said: They are like little children who have settled in a field that is not theirs. When the owners of the field come, they will say, "Give our field back to us." They strip in front of them to give it back, and they give them their field. Therefore I say: if the master of the house knows the thief is coming, he will keep watch before he comes and not let him break into the house of his domain to carry off his goods. You, then, keep watch against the world; gird your loins with great strength, so that the robbers find no way to reach you, for the trouble you expect will come. Let there be among you a person of understanding. When the fruit ripened, he came quickly with his sickle in his hand and reaped it. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.

22Jesus saw little ones taking milk. He said to his disciples: These little ones taking milk are like those who go into the kingdom. They said to him: Then will we go into the kingdom as little ones? Jesus said to them: When you make the two one, and make the inside like the outside and the outside like the inside and the above like the below, and when you make the male and the female into a single one, so that the male is not male and the female not female; when you make eyes in place of an eye, and a hand in place of a hand, and a foot in place of a foot, an image in place of an image, then you will go into [the kingdom].

23Jesus said: I will choose you, one out of a thousand and two out of ten thousand, and they will stand as a single one.

24His disciples said: Show us the place where you are, since we must seek it. He said to them: Whoever has ears, let him hear. There is light inside a person of light, and he gives light to the whole world. If he does not give light, there is darkness.

25Jesus said: Love your brother like your soul; guard him like the pupil of your eye.

26Jesus said: The speck in your brother's eye you see, but the beam in your own eye you do not see. When you throw the beam out of your eye, then you will see clearly to throw the speck out of your brother's eye.

27If you do not fast from the world, you will not find the kingdom. If you do not make the Sabbath a Sabbath, you will not see the father.

28Jesus said: I stood in the middle of the world, and I appeared to them in flesh. I found them all drunk; I found none of them thirsty. And my soul was pained for the children of men, because they are blind in their hearts and do not see; for empty they came into the world, and empty they seek to go out of the world again. But now they are drunk. When they shake off their wine, then they will change their minds.

29Jesus said: If the flesh came to be because of spirit, it is a wonder. But if spirit came to be because of the body, it is a wonder of wonders. Yet I marvel at this: how this great wealth has made its home in this poverty.

30Jesus said: Where there are three gods, they are gods. Where there are two or one, I am with him. (The Greek reads: where there are three, they are without God; where there is one alone, I say I am with him; and it continues with the wood and the stone of saying 77.)

31Jesus said: No prophet is accepted in his own village; no physician heals those who know him.

32Jesus said: A city built on a high mountain and fortified cannot fall, nor can it be hidden.

33Jesus said: What you will hear in your ear, in the other ear proclaim from your rooftops. For no one lights a lamp and puts it under a basket, nor puts it in a hidden place; rather he sets it on the lampstand so that everyone who goes in and comes out will see its light.

34Jesus said: If a blind man leads a blind man, the two of them fall into a pit.

35Jesus said: No one can go into the strong man's house and take it by force unless he binds his hands; then he will plunder his house.

36Jesus said: Do not be anxious from morning to evening and from evening to morning about what you will put on. (The Greek is longer: food as well as clothing, and the lilies that neither card nor spin.)

37His disciples said: On what day will you appear to us, and on what day will we see you? Jesus said: When you strip off your shame, and take your clothes and put them under your feet like little children and trample them, then you will see the son of the living one, and you will not be afraid.

38Jesus said: Many times you have longed to hear these words that I say to you, and you have no one else to hear them from. There will be days when you will seek me and not find me.

39Jesus said: The Pharisees and the scribes have taken the keys of knowledge and hidden them. They did not go in, and those who wanted to go in they did not allow. You, though, be wise as serpents and innocent as doves.

40Jesus said: A vine has been planted outside the father, and since it is not strong, it will be pulled up by its root and perish.

41Jesus said: Whoever has something in his hand will be given more; and whoever has nothing, even the little he has will be taken from him.

42Jesus said: Become passers-by.

43His disciples said to him: Who are you, that you say these things to us? From what I say to you, you do not know who I am; rather, you have become like the Jews, for they love the tree and hate its fruit, and they love the fruit and hate the tree.

44Jesus said: Whoever blasphemes the father will be forgiven, and whoever blasphemes the son will be forgiven; but whoever blasphemes the holy spirit will not be forgiven, either on earth or in the sky.

45Jesus said: Grapes are not harvested from thorns, nor figs gathered from thistles, for they give no fruit. A good man brings good out of his store; a bad man brings bad things out of his bad store, which is in his heart, and speaks bad things. For out of the overflow of the heart he brings out bad things.

46Jesus said: From Adam to John the Baptist, among those born of women none is higher than John the Baptist, so that his eyes should not be lowered. But I have said: whoever among you becomes small will know the kingdom and will be higher than John.

47Jesus said: A man cannot mount two horses or draw two bows, and a servant cannot serve two masters, or he will honor the one and insult the other. No one drinks old wine and at once wants to drink new wine. New wine is not put into old skins, lest they burst; and old wine is not put into a new skin, lest it spoil it. An old patch is not sewn on a new garment, since a tear would come.

48Jesus said: If two make peace with each other in this one house, they will say to the mountain, "Move away," and it will move.

49Jesus said: Blessed are the solitary and the chosen, for you will find the kingdom; for you are from it, and you will go back there.

50Jesus said: If they say to you, "Where have you come from?" say to them, "We have come from the light, the place where the light came to be by itself alone; it stood, and it appeared in their image." If they say to you, "Is it you?" say, "We are its children, and we are the chosen of the living father." If they ask you, "What is the sign of your father in you?" say to them, "It is a movement and a rest."

51His disciples said to him: On what day will the rest of the dead come, and on what day will the new world come? He said to them: What you look for has come, but you do not know it.

52His disciples said to him: Twenty-four prophets spoke in Israel, and they all spoke in you. He said to them: You have left out the living one in front of you and have spoken about the dead.

53His disciples said to him: Is circumcision useful or not? He said to them: If it were useful, their father would beget them from their mother already circumcised. But the true circumcision in spirit has found all its profit.

54Jesus said: Blessed are the poor, for yours is the kingdom of the skies.

55Jesus said: Whoever does not hate his father and his mother cannot become my disciple; and whoever does not hate his brothers and his sisters and carry his cross as I do will not be worthy of me.

56Jesus said: Whoever has known the world has found a corpse; and whoever has found a corpse, the world is not worthy of him.

57Jesus said: The kingdom of the father is like a man who had good seed. His enemy came by night and sowed weeds among the good seed. The man did not let them pull up the weeds. He said to them: lest you go to pull up the weeds and pull up the wheat with them. For on the day of the harvest the weeds will be plain; they will be pulled up and burned.

58Jesus said: Blessed is the man who has toiled; he has found life.

59Jesus said: Look to the living one while you are alive, lest you die and seek to see him and be unable to see.

60They saw a Samaritan carrying a lamb, going into Judea. He said to his disciples: That man is around the lamb. They said to him: So that he may kill it and eat it. He said to them: While it is alive he will not eat it, but only if he kills it and it becomes a corpse. They said: Otherwise he cannot do it. He said to them: You too, seek a place for yourselves in rest, lest you become a corpse and be eaten.

61Jesus said: Two will rest on a couch; the one will die, the one will live. Salome said: Who are you, man? As if from someone, you have climbed on my couch and eaten from my table. Jesus said to her: I am the one who is from what is equal; I was given some of the things of my father. (Salome:) I am your disciple. (Jesus:) Therefore I say: when someone is equal, he will be filled with light; but when he is divided, he will be filled with darkness.

62Jesus said: I tell my mysteries to those [who are worthy of my] mysteries. What your right hand will do, do not let your left hand know what it does.

63Jesus said: There was a rich man who had much money. He said: I will use my money to sow and reap and plant and fill my storehouses with fruit, so that I lack nothing. These were the things he thought in his heart, and in that night he died. Whoever has ears, let him hear.

64Jesus said: A man had guests, and when he had prepared the dinner he sent his servant to invite the guests. He went to the first and said to him: My master invites you. He said: I have money owed me by some merchants; they are coming to me this evening; I must go and give them orders. I excuse myself from the dinner. He went to another and said to him: My master has invited you. He said to him: I have bought a house and they want me for a day; I will have no time. He came to another and said to him: My master invites you. He said to him: My friend is going to marry, and I am to prepare the meal; I cannot come. I excuse myself from the dinner. He went to another and said to him: My master invites you. He said to him: I have bought a village; I am going to collect the rent; I cannot come. I excuse myself. The servant came and said to his master: Those you invited to the dinner have excused themselves. The master said to his servant: Go out to the roads; those you find, bring them to dine. Buyers and merchants [will] not go into the places of my father.

65He said: A good man had a vineyard. He gave it to tenant farmers so that they would work it and he would take its fruit from them. He sent his servant so that the farmers would give him the fruit of the vineyard. They seized his servant and beat him; a little more and they would have killed him. The servant went and told his master. His master said: Perhaps he did not know them. He sent another servant; the farmers beat that one too. Then the master sent his son. He said: Perhaps they will respect my son. Since those farmers knew that he was the heir of the vineyard, they seized him and killed him. Whoever has ears, let him hear. ("Good" is a restoration; the damaged word may instead be "creditor.")

66Jesus said: Show me the stone that the builders rejected; it is the cornerstone.

67Jesus said: Whoever knows the all but lacks himself lacks the whole place.

68Jesus said: Blessed are you when you are hated and persecuted, and no place will be found where you have been persecuted.

69Jesus said: Blessed are those who have been persecuted in their heart; they are the ones who have known the father in truth. Blessed are the hungry, so that the belly of whoever wants will be filled.

70Jesus said: When you bring forth that which is within you, what you have will save you. If you do not have that within you, what you do not have within you [will] kill you.

71Jesus said: I will destroy [this] house, and no one will be able to build it [again].

72[A man said] to him: Tell my brothers to divide my father's things with me. He said to him: Man, who made me a divider? He turned to his disciples and said to them: Am I a divider?

73Jesus said: The harvest is great but the workers few. So beg the lord to send workers out to the harvest.

74He said: Lord, there are many around the well, but nothing in the well.

75Jesus said: Many stand at the door, but it is the solitary who will go into the bridal chamber.

76Jesus said: The kingdom of the father is like a merchant who had a load of goods and found a pearl. That merchant was wise: he sold the load and bought the single pearl for himself. You too, seek the treasure that does not fail, that lasts, where no moth comes near to eat and no worm destroys.

77Jesus said: I am the light that is over all of them. I am the all; the all came out of me, and the all reached me. Split a piece of wood: I am there. Lift the stone, and you will find me there. (In the Greek the wood and the stone follow saying 30.)

78Jesus said: Why have you come out to the field? To see a reed shaken by the wind? And to see a man with soft clothing on him, [like your] kings and your great men? Those have the soft clothing on them, and they cannot know the truth.

79A woman in the crowd said to him: Blessed is the womb that carried you and the breasts that fed you. He said to her: Blessed are those who have heard the word of the father and kept it in truth. For there will be days when you will say: blessed is the womb that has not conceived and the breasts that have not given milk.

80Jesus said: Whoever has known the world has found the body; and whoever has found the body, the world is not worthy of him.

81Jesus said: Whoever has become rich, let him be king; and whoever has power, let him renounce.

82Jesus said: Whoever is near me is near the fire, and whoever is far from me is far from the kingdom.

83Jesus said: The images are visible to man, and the light that is in them is hidden in the image of the light of the father. He will be revealed, and his image is hidden by his light.

84Jesus said: On the days when you see your likeness, you rejoice. But when you see your images that came to be before you, which neither die nor are revealed, how much will you bear?

85Jesus said: Adam came to be from a great power and a great wealth, and he did not become worthy of you. For had he been worthy, [he would not have tasted] death.

86Jesus said: [The foxes have their dens] and the birds have their nest, but the son of man has no place to lay his head and rest.

87Jesus said: Wretched is the body that hangs on a body, and wretched is the soul that hangs on these two.

88Jesus said: The messengers are coming to you with the prophets, and they will give you what is yours. And you too, give them what is in your hands, and say to yourselves: On what day are they coming to take what is theirs?

89Jesus said: Why do you wash the outside of the cup? Do you not understand that the one who made the inside is also the one who made the outside?

90Jesus said: Come to me, for my yoke is easy and my lordship is gentle, and you will find rest for yourselves.

91They said to him: Tell us who you are, so that we may believe in you. He said to them: You test the face of the sky and the earth, and the one in front of you you have not known, and this moment you do not know how to test.

92Jesus said: Seek and you will find. But the things you asked me about in those days, which I did not tell you then, now I want to tell them, and you do not seek them.

93Do not give what is holy to the dogs, lest they throw it on the dung heap. Do not throw the pearls to the pigs, lest they make it [...].

94Jesus [said]: Whoever seeks will find; [whoever knocks] will be opened to.

95[Jesus said:] If you have money, do not lend at interest; rather give [it] to the one from whom you will not get it back.

96Jesus [said]: The kingdom of the father is like a woman. She took a little leaven, [hid] it in dough, and made it into large loaves. Whoever has ears, let him hear.

97Jesus said: The kingdom of the [father] is like a woman carrying a jar full of meal. While she was walking on a long road, the handle of the jar broke; the meal poured out behind her on the road. She did not know it; she had noticed no trouble. When she reached her house, she set the jar down and found it empty.

98Jesus said: The kingdom of the father is like a man who wanted to kill a great man. He drew the sword in his house and drove it into the wall, to know whether his hand would hold. Then he killed the great man.

99The disciples said to him: Your brothers and your mother are standing outside. He said to them: Those here who do the will of my father, these are my brothers and my mother. They are the ones who will go into the kingdom of my father.

100They showed Jesus a gold piece and said to him: Caesar's people demand taxes from us. He said to them: Give Caesar's to Caesar, give God's to God, and what is mine, give it to me.

101Whoever does not hate his [father] and his mother as I do cannot be my [disciple]; and whoever does [not] love his [father and] his mother as I do cannot be my [disciple]. For my mother [...], but [my] true [mother] gave me life.

102Jesus said: Woe to the Pharisees, for they are like a dog lying in the cattle's manger: it neither eats nor lets the cattle eat.

103Jesus said: Blessed is the man who knows at [what] part the robbers are coming in, so that he will rise, gather his [domain], and gird his loins before they come in.

104They said to [Jesus]: Come, let us pray today and fast. Jesus said: What sin have I done, or in what have I been beaten? But when the bridegroom comes out of the bridal chamber, then let them fast and pray.

105Jesus said: Whoever knows the father and the mother will be called the son of a whore.

106Jesus said: When you make the two one, you will become sons of man, and when you say, "Mountain, move away," it will move.

107Jesus said: The kingdom is like a shepherd who had a hundred sheep. One of them, the largest, strayed. He left the ninety-nine and sought the one until he found it. Having toiled, he said to the sheep: I love you more than the ninety-nine.

108Jesus said: Whoever drinks from my mouth will become like me; I myself will become him, and the hidden things will be revealed to him.

109Jesus said: The kingdom is like a man who had a treasure [hidden] in his field without knowing it. And [after] he died he left it to his [son]. The son did not know; he took that field and sold [it]. And the buyer came, and while plowing [found] the treasure. He began to lend money at interest to whomever he wished.

110Jesus said: Whoever has found the world and become rich, let him renounce the world.

111Jesus said: The skies will roll up, and the earth, in front of you, and the one who lives from the living one will not see death. Does not Jesus say: Whoever finds himself, the world is not worthy of him?

112Jesus said: Woe to the flesh that hangs on the soul; woe to the soul that hangs on the flesh.

113His disciples said to him: On what day is the kingdom coming? It is not coming by watching for it. They will not say, "Look, here," or "Look, there." Rather, the kingdom of the father is spread out over the earth, and people do not see it.

114Simon Peter said to them: Let Mary go out from among us, for women are not worthy of life. Jesus said: Look, I will draw her so as to make her male, so that she too may become a living spirit like you males. For every woman who makes herself male will go into the kingdom of the skies.

The Gospel according to Thomas.
